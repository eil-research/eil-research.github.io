Efficient Inference and Learning Lab

Welcome to the homepage of the Efficient Inference and Learning Lab in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at Korea University, directed by Sejun Park.

We solve problems in machine learning caused by finite-precision arithmetic, the arithmetic computers actually use: what models can and cannot express, whether training and generalization guarantees still hold, and the security, privacy, and verification failures that rounding creates. Please visit the Research Interests page for more details.
